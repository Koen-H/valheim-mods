# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Fishing indicator under the crosshair: says when a fish bites, when to reel in a hooked fish and when to
  wait while it fights. Turn it off with `Indicator` in the new Fishing section of the config.
- `TetraInDeepNorth` (off by default): chum can also bring Tetra in the Deep North.
- `HandCatchCounts` (off by default): picking up a fish by hand counts as catching it.

## 1.0.0

- First release: fish and bait tooltips for The Fisherman, and chum to lure the fish of a biome to you.
