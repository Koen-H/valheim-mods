# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- The map toggles now look like the Cartography Table toggle and sit above it, instead of copying the
  "Visible to other players" toggle.
- New "Show whole map" toggle (off by default): lifts the fog from the big map while it is on, to look for
  mountains. What you explored doesn't change.

## 1.0.0

- First release: the highest peaks on the big map with how much to build on top, and messages for your
  highest and tallest build, for Top of the World and Mighty Halls.
